02Our Services

We offer website design, website redesigns, a monthly Website Care Plan (which includes hosting), search engine optimization (SEO) and local citations, Google Ads management, Facebook and Instagram ads management, and graphic design. What's included in each website or service is described on our website, and any custom details will be confirmed with you by phone, text or email before work begins.

03Pricing and Payment

  • Website setup is a one-time price, paid before work begins: $99 for a 5-page website or $199 for a 10-page website, unless a different price is agreed in writing. The Website Care Plan ($59 per month, billed automatically) is started separately from your confirmation page after you pay for your website setup.
  • Website redesigns are custom quoted based on your current website, and you will see your price before you pay anything.
  • Monthly services (SEO, Google Ads management and Facebook and Instagram ads management) are $199 per month each, billed in advance.
  • The Website Care Plan (hosting, security, updates and small edits) is $59 per month, billed monthly, and is included with every website we build. There is no contract.
  • Graphic design is priced per project. Most projects start at $75, and we'll confirm your exact price before any work begins. Your first logo is free with every website.
  • Payments are processed by Square. Prices may change in the future, but we'll never change the price of something you've already paid for.

04Our 7-Day Delivery Promise

Speed is one of the things we're proudest of, so we back it with our money.

  • The 7 days begin once we have everything we need from you. That means your business details, plus your logo, wording and photos if you are providing them. If you would like us to create those for you, we can begin as soon as we have your business details and your payment. We will tell you the day your 7 days begin.
  • Your website will be fully designed within 7 days. "Fully designed" means your website is built and ready for your review.
  • If we don't deliver within those 7 days, the money you paid for your website setup comes back to you.
  • This promise is our commitment to on-time delivery. Changes you request after your review, and connecting your web address, happen after the 7 days and aren't part of the countdown.

05Your Website Project

  • Free preview: before you pay, we will build your homepage so you can see it. You are not required to buy after seeing it, and you owe nothing for the preview.
  • Content: free professional writing and photos are included with every website. If you give us your own text, photos or logos, you confirm you have the right to use them.
  • Your review: you'll have a chance to review your website and request changes before it goes live. Reasonable changes to the work we've created are included. Brand-new pages or features beyond your website may cost extra (additional pages are $40 each), and we'll always tell you first.
  • Free extras: the free logo design (3 designs to choose from) and the creation of 2 brand-new business social media pages are included with every website. Ongoing posting and management of those pages is not included.
  • Your domain: you buy and own your web address (domain name) from a registrar of your choice. We'll help you connect it to your website. If you don't have one yet, we will help you choose one.
  • Delays: if a project is paused for more than 30 days because we're waiting on you, we may close it. You can restart it by contacting us.
  • Portfolio: we may show your finished website in our portfolio and marketing. If you'd prefer we didn't, just let us know.

06Monthly Services and Cancellation

  • There are no long-term contracts. You can cancel any monthly service, including the Website Care Plan, at any time by calling, texting or emailing us.
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. Your service keeps running through the month you've already paid for, and you won't be billed again.
  • The Website Care Plan covers hosting, the security lock (SSL), updates and upkeep, and small edits such as text, image, hours and contact changes, with no per-edit fee. Requests are made through our update request form, by text or by phone. Larger changes, like new pages or new features, are quoted separately.
  • If you cancel the Care Plan, hosting ends at the end of your paid period. You keep your domain, your logo and your social media pages, and on request we will send you your website's files so you can host them elsewhere.

  • Large online stores (100 or more products)
  • Advanced custom integrations

  • Ongoing social media management
  • Ongoing content writing
  • SEO and advertising services (our monthly SEO, Google Ads and Facebook and Instagram ads services are separate, optional and $199 per month each)
  • Your domain name registration and your advertising budgets
