Google Ads in plain English

What Are Google Ads?

When you search on Google, the very first results often have a small label that says "Sponsored." Those are Google Ads. Businesses pay per click to appear there, right at the top, above everyone else.

Here's the best part: you only pay Google when someone actually clicks on your ad to visit your website or call you. If nobody clicks, you don't pay for that ad being shown.

Think of it like renting the best billboard in town, but one that only appears in front of people who are searching for exactly what you sell, at the exact moment they need it.

Here's where it gets good

Once SEO Kicks In, You Show Up Twice on Page One

At first, your ad does all the work. It sits at the very top of Google, so people start calling right away. Meanwhile, our monthly SEO work is quietly lifting your website up the regular, unpaid results below the ads.

Then, once SEO kicks in, something great happens: your business appears in two places on page one at the same time, in the ad at the top and again in the regular results.

  • More of page one is yours. Every spot you hold is one less spot for a competitor.
  • Customers see you twice. A name that shows up twice looks established and trustworthy, so more people click it.
  • One covers for the other. Slow month and you pause your ads? SEO still keeps you in front of customers. Rankings shift for a week? Your ads keep the phone ringing.

Google Ads Questions, Answered

Does the $199 a month include my ad budget?

No. The $199 is our fee to set up your ads and put in the many hours of work it takes every month to manage, test and improve them, so every dollar you spend works as hard as possible. Your ad budget is paid directly to Google, and you decide how much to spend.

Is Google Ads a good fit for a brand-new business?

Yes. Ads don't depend on how long you've been around or how many reviews you have, so a new business can appear right alongside established ones. We make sure your website is ready to turn those clicks into calls.

I've tried Google Ads before and it didn't work. What's different?

Ads can waste a lot of money when they aren't set up and managed carefully. We research your searches, block wasted clicks and fine-tune your ads every month, so your budget works harder than it may have before.

How soon will I get calls?

Ads can begin appearing the same day they go live, so calls and website visits can start right away. Whether you're new or established, we keep improving them month after month.

How much should I spend on ads?

That's up to you, and you can start small. I’ll recommend a budget based on your business, your area and your goals, and you can raise or lower it anytime.

Do I need a website to run Google Ads?

Yes. Every click goes to your website, so a professional website makes a huge difference. If you don't have one, or yours needs updating, we can build or redesign it.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no long contracts. You can cancel whenever you like.
