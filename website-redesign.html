The questions everyone asks

Redesigning Is Easier Than You Think

So why do so many owners wait? Most put off a redesign because they're worried about what could go wrong. Here's the truth.

Will I keep my web address?

Yes. Your web address (like yourbusiness.com) stays the same. Customers find you exactly where they always have.

What about my Google rankings?

If you've already earned rankings, I plan the switch carefully to protect them. If your site hasn't been showing up yet, the new one is built from the start to climb.

Will my website go down during the switch?

No. We build your new website behind the scenes and switch it over when it's ready, so customers never see a broken page.

What happens to my current content?

We keep what's working, rewrite what isn't, and add professional writing and photos wherever they'll help.

Do I have to do a lot of work?

Not at all. One quick call to learn about your business, then a chance to review before launch. I handle the rest.

Custom pricing

Every Redesign Is Quoted to Fit Your Website

No two websites are alike, so a redesign never has a one-size-fits-all price. Rebuilding a website that already exists takes planning, and the work depends on what you have and what you need. That is why I look at your website first and give you a custom price. Here is what shapes it.

How many pages you have today

A 4-page website and a 40-page website are very different jobs. I look at what you have, and we decide together which pages to keep, combine or add.

What you need it to do

Contact forms, booking, galleries, menus or a small store. The more your website has to do, the more it takes to build it right.

What has to move over

Blog posts, product listings, photos and old pages that bring in visitors. I protect whatever is already earning you traffic.

Where it’s built today

Some platforms move over easily and others need a full rebuild. Knowing yours helps me give you an accurate price.

Website Redesign Questions

How much does a redesign cost?

Every redesign is custom quoted, because no two websites are alike. The price depends on how many pages you have now, what you need on the new website, what has to move over, and what platform it's built on. You get your exact price before you pay anything, and the quote is free.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text me, or fill out the form, and tell me your web address. I'll look over your website and call you within 30 minutes to talk it through. Then you get a clear, custom price with the timeline agreed up front.

I don't see my platform. Can you still redesign my website?

Yes. I work with many more platforms than I can list. Send me your web address and I'll tell you what it's built on and what a redesign looks like. If you're not sure what you're using, that's fine, I can find out.

Does it cost more if my website has a lot of pages?

More pages usually means more work, and that shows up in your custom quote. Tell me how many pages you have today. We'll decide together which to keep, combine or drop, and I'll price it from there.

I only built my website recently. Is a redesign worth it?

Many owners build a first website themselves to get started, and that's a great first step. If yours isn't bringing in calls, a redesign adds professional design, new writing and photos, and a setup made for Google.

Is a redesign different from a brand-new website?

Yes. A new website starts from nothing, so it has simple set prices of $99 and $199. A redesign starts with what you already have, so I keep what's working and fix what isn't. That is why redesigns are custom quoted. No website at all? See the new website page.

Can I keep my logo and colors?

Yes, if you love them. Want a fresh logo too? I design logos as well, and your quote can include one.

Will my web address and Google rankings be safe?

Yes. You keep your web address, and I plan the switch carefully to protect any rankings you've already earned.

What if I only need a few changes, not a full redesign?

No problem. Leave your number and I’ll call you within 30 minutes to talk through what makes the most sense.

Can you host my new website?

Yes. The Website Care Plan is $59 a month and covers your hosting, so your new site stays fast, secure and online. See the Website Care Plan.

Who owns my domain name?

You do. You buy your own web address (called a domain) from a registrar, usually for about $10 to $20 a year, so it always belongs to you. I help you pick one and connect it to your new website. Already have a domain? I’ll connect that one.

What happens if I cancel?

You can cancel anytime, with no contract and no cancellation fee. Your Care Plan ends at the end of the month you've paid for. You keep your domain, your logo and your social media pages, and on request I’ll send you your website's files so you can host them elsewhere.
