Questions About Pricing

How does the free preview work?

Call or fill out the form, and I’ll call you within 30 minutes. Tell me about your business, and I build your homepage first. You see it, tell me what you'd change, and only then decide. If you love it, you pay the setup price and I build the rest. If you don't, you owe nothing.

Is paying online safe?

Yes. All payments go through Square, one of the most trusted payment companies in the country. Your card details are never shared with me.

What happens after I pay?

I’ll call you within 30 minutes to learn about your business and collect what I need, and I get right to work. Your 7 days begin once I have everything I need from you, like your logo, wording and photos if you’re providing them.

How do I pay?

You have two easy options. You can click Buy Now to pay the setup price safely online through Square, a trusted payment company, using any credit or debit card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Or, if you'd rather see your free homepage preview first, call or text me at 813-556-5611. I’ll answer your questions, help you pick 5 or 10 pages, and send you a secure payment link by text or email whenever you're ready. No pressure either way.

I’m just starting my business, or I don't have a website. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Many of our clients are opening their doors for the first time or have never had a website. We build your whole website from scratch, including the writing and photos, and guide you through every step in plain English. You don't need reviews, a long history or any website know-how to get started.

Can I start small and grow later?

Absolutely. Many owners begin with a 5-page website and grow from there, adding pages, SEO or ads whenever they're ready. Whether you're brand new or have been in business for years, you decide the pace.

What counts as a page?

A page is one section of your website that people can visit, like your Home page, About Us, Services, Photo Gallery or Contact page. Each page gives Google another way to find you.

What comes free with my website?

Both the 5-page and 10-page websites include a free logo (you pick your favorite from 3 designs), 2 brand-new business social media pages that we create for you, like Facebook and Instagram, and free professional writing and photos. You also see your homepage free before you pay.

What does the $59 a month cover, and is there a contract?

Your website is a one-time setup price, $99 for 5 pages or $199 for 10 pages. The $59-a-month Website Care Plan keeps it online: hosting, fast loading, the security lock (SSL), updates and small edits, and help with your web address. There is no contract, so you can cancel anytime. Our marketing services, like SEO, Google Ads and Facebook and Instagram ads, are separate, optional, and $199 a month each.

Who owns my domain name?

You do. You buy your own web address (called a domain) from a registrar, usually for about $10 to $20 a year, so it always belongs to you. I help you pick one and connect it to your new website. Already have a domain? I’ll connect that one.

What isn't included in the price?

To keep pricing simple and affordable, a few things are not included, but I’m happy to talk about custom pricing for them: complex custom web applications, large online stores (100+ products), advanced custom integrations, custom photo and video shoots, ongoing social media management, ongoing content writing, and SEO and ads (those are $199 a month each). Your domain name is bought by you, and ad budgets are paid directly to Google or Facebook.

What happens if I cancel?

You can cancel anytime, with no contract and no cancellation fee. Your Care Plan ends at the end of the month you've paid for. You keep your domain, your logo and your social media pages, and on request I’ll send you your website's files so you can host them elsewhere.
