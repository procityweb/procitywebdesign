Before You Reach Out

How does the free preview work?

Call or fill out the form, and I’ll call you within 30 minutes. Tell me about your business, and I build your homepage first. You see it, tell me what you'd change, and only then decide. If you love it, you pay the setup price and I build the rest. If you don't, you owe nothing.

I’m not good with computers. Is that a problem?

Not at all. You don't need to know anything about websites or computers. I handle the design, the writing, the photos and the technical setup. You just tell me about your business, and I take it from there.

What happens after I pay?

I’ll call you within 30 minutes to learn about your business and collect what I need, and I get right to work. Your 7 days begin once I have everything I need from you, like your logo, wording and photos if you’re providing them.

Who owns my domain name?

You do. You buy your own web address (called a domain) from a registrar, usually for about $10 to $20 a year, so it always belongs to you. I help you pick one and connect it to your new website. Already have a domain? I’ll connect that one.

Do you work with businesses outside my area?

Yes. I build websites for businesses all across the country, and everything is handled by phone, text and email.

How do I pay?

You have two easy options. You can click Buy Now to pay the setup price safely online through Square, a trusted payment company, using any credit or debit card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Or, if you'd rather see your free homepage preview first, call or text me at 813-556-5611. I’ll answer your questions, help you pick 5 or 10 pages, and send you a secure payment link by text or email whenever you're ready. No pressure either way.
