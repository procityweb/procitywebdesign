Why social media ads work

Your Customers Are Already Scrolling. Let's Make Sure They See You.

Google is where people go when they already know they need something. Facebook and Instagram are where you get noticed before they even start searching.

Picture a homeowner scrolling on a Sunday night. They see your ad for a spring special, tap "like," send you a message, and book you on Monday. They weren't even looking for you. Your ad found them.

That's the power of social media ads: you choose exactly who sees your business, and you stay on their minds until they're ready to call.

Social Media Ads Questions, Answered

Does the $199 a month include my ad budget?

No. The $199 is our fee to set up your ads and put in the many hours of work it takes every month to manage, test and improve them, so every dollar you spend works as hard as possible. Your ad budget is paid directly to Google, or to Facebook and Instagram, and you decide how much to spend. Not sure where to start? I’ll recommend a budget that fits your business.

I’m brand new and have no followers. Will anyone see my ads?

Yes. Ads are shown to people near you who fit your ideal customer, so you don't need followers to get started. In fact, ads are one of the fastest ways to build your following.

How soon will I see results?

Your ads start reaching people the day they go live, so likes, comments and messages often begin right away. Calls and new customers build from there as more people see your business and we improve your ads.

How much should I spend on ads?

That's up to you, and you can start small. I’ll recommend a budget based on your business, your area and your goals, and you can raise or lower it anytime.

Do I need a Facebook or Instagram page first?

You'll need one to run ads, but don't worry. If you don't have them, we can set them up for you. We create 2 brand-new social media pages free with every website we build.

Should I do Facebook ads or Google Ads?

They work great together. Google Ads reach people who are already searching for what you do. Facebook and Instagram ads reach people before they start searching. I can help you decide what fits your business best.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no long contracts. You can cancel whenever you like.
