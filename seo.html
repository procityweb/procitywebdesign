SEO in plain English

What Is SEO, and Why Should You Care?

SEO stands for search engine optimization. A search engine, like Google or Bing, is where people type what they need and get a list of businesses to choose from. SEO is everything we do to help your business show up near the top of that list. That means setting up your Google Maps listing, getting your business listed on trusted websites like Yelp and the BBB (these listings are called local citations), and making sure your website pages include the same words your customers type.

Think about the last time you needed a plumber, a hair stylist or a place to eat. You searched on Google, looked at the first few results and picked one. You probably never went to page 2. Most people don't.

That means if your business isn't near the top, those customers are likely calling someone else.

How Google decides who shows up first

Google Is a Careful Judge. Here's What It Asks.

So how does Google decide who gets the top spot? A brand-new website is invisible until Google trusts it. Before Google recommends any business, it looks for answers to four questions. SEO is how we make sure the answer is always "yes."

  1. Does it clearly say who you are and what you do? Does the website tell your story and explain your services, whether you opened last month or 20 years ago?
  2. Does it know what it's talking about? Does it clearly explain each service people are searching for?
  3. Is it listed in the right places? Is the business found on trusted websites like the BBB, Yelp and more? (These listings are called local citations.)
  4. Can people trust it? Is it secure, easy to use on a phone and easy to contact?

Our secret weapon: local citations

The Secret Weapon Most Businesses Never Use

Remember Google's third question, "Is it listed in the right places?" Local citations are the answer, and they're the part most businesses skip. A local citation is any place online that lists your business name, address and phone number. Think Yelp, Bing, Apple Maps, the Better Business Bureau, Yellow Pages, Nextdoor and many more. We can get your business listed on up to 250 of them.

Here's why that's a big deal: Google uses these listings to double-check your business. Every time Google finds your business listed the same way on a trusted website, it's like another person vouching for you.

Think of it like a job interview. If one person tells the hiring manager you're great, that's nice. If fifty trusted people all say the exact same great things about you, you get the job. Citations are those references, and Google is the hiring manager. Now imagine having up to 250 trusted websites all vouching for your business.

SEO Questions, Answered

I’m brand new. Is SEO still worth it?

Yes, and the earlier the better. A new business starts with no listings and no history on Google, so SEO sets up your Google Maps listing and directory listings from the start, instead of years later.

I've been in business for years. What will SEO change?

Even a well-known business can be hard to find online. SEO makes sure your listings match everywhere, your pages use the words customers search, and Google keeps getting fresh reasons to show you.

How long does SEO take to work?

Every business and every town is different, but most clients see movement within a few months, as the chart above shows. SEO keeps building over time, so the longer it runs, the stronger your results usually become.

What do I need to get started?

Just your business name, address and phone number, plus a quick call with me. I handle the rest, whether you already have a website or need one built.

Can you guarantee I’ll be #1 on Google?

No honest company can promise a specific spot, because Google makes the final decision. What we can promise is real work every month to keep you climbing.

Do I need a new website for SEO to work?

Not always. We can work with the website you have. But if your current site is outdated or hard to use on a phone, a new website or a redesign will help your SEO work much faster.
